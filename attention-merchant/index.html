ATTENTION MERCHANT

You are the product lead for the feed at The Commons ○ fictional platform — a social network of about forty million people. You do not write posts. You do not ban posts. You set four numbers: the ranking weights the feed uses to decide what each person sees next.

The board wants cumulative net revenue of $420M ◇ target over eight quarters. Advertisers pay for engagement. Engagement is whatever your weights say it is.

Two ledgers run side by side for the whole game. Ledger A is the one you are paid on. Ledger B is the one everybody has to live in.

How a quarter works

1. Split 100 weight points across four signals: Watch Time, Shares, Comments, and Surveyed Trust.
2. Set the Trust & Safety budget — it costs revenue and blunts damage.
3. Run the quarter. The feed is built from your weights ＝ calculated, the money arrives, and Information Health moves.

Every post type, profile, and dollar figure in this game is a labelled scenario assumption ◇ or fiction ○. The one real-world anchor is in the credits, marked ◆. Nobody in this game will ever ask you to lie, fabricate a post, or ban a viewpoint. Watch what the weights do on their own.

Credits & sources

◆ Sourced: reporting on internal company research (The Wall Street Journal, “The Facebook Files,” 2021) described engagement-based ranking amplifying outrage and divisive content.
◇ Assumed: all post-archetype profiles, base shares, dollar rates, the $420M board target, thresholds, and event sizes are scenario parameters, shown in full during play.
＝ Calculated: every ledger figure is arithmetic on those parameters, using the formulas printed in the debrief.
○ Fictional: The Commons, its users, its board, and every post are invented.

The pool this quarter ◇

What exists to be ranked. Affinity = how strongly a post type responds when you weight that signal (0–10). Health = what amplifying it does to Information Health, per unit of reach.

Ranking weights — spend exactly 100 points

Points allocated: 100 / 100

Trust & Safety budget ◇

Moderation, labelling, and review staff. Charged against revenue. Blunts the Information Health damage done by outrage and conspiracy reach — and softens a viral falsehood, if one comes.

Scoring, printed in full: each post type's boost ＝ (Σ weight × affinity) / 100. Reach ＝ base × (0.2 + boost / 5), normalised to 100 units of feed. Engagement ＝ Σ reach × engagement rate. Gross revenue ＝ engagement × $0.12M ◇. A growth bonus of $8M ◇ pays in any quarter where engagement beats the quarter before. Information Health moves 3.5 × Σ (reach × health) / 100, with negative contributions reduced by your Trust & Safety level.
