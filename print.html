One-page playbook

The Plebeian Reclamation, on one page

The philosophy (Dynamic Synthesism): governance is an operational problem. Judge ideas by whether they work; judge leaders on Sophistry vs. Stewardship; treat parties as infrastructure, never identity.

Phase 1 — Geographic Audit. Get the roster and the bylaws. Count total seats, filled seats, vacant seats.

Phase 2 — Quorum & Seat Acquisition. Do the quorum math. Bring three to five serious people; claim the vacant seats by filing and attending.

Phase 3 — The Clean-Money Floor Vote. Move a binding bylaw: the committee designates entities grossly misaligned with its platform; the committee, its auxiliaries, and its candidates take none of their money; the list is public; bad contributions are refunded in 30 days.

Proof: Lonoke County, Arkansas — four people, a quorum, §601.4 adopted January 23, 2026.
