Model Bylaw: Prohibited Corporate Contributions

For Adoption by County Central Committees

"This is the baseline language we used to protect our county committee in Lonoke County from corporate money[cite: 2]. You don't need a high-priced lawyer to pass this. You just need to bring it up under new business, move to adopt, and force a roll-call vote." — Corey[cite: 1]

ARTICLE [X]: PROHIBITED CONTRIBUTIONS AND FINANCIAL INTEGRITY

Section 1. Intent.
The intent of this article is to ensure that the Committee remains accountable strictly to the citizens and working families of [Insert County Name], free from the influence of corporate political action committees and paid lobbyists[cite: 2].

Section 2. Definitions.
(a) "Corporate PAC" means any political action committee established, funded, or controlled by a for-profit corporation, trade association, or corporate entity.
(b) "Registered Lobbyist" means any individual registered to lobby municipal, county, state, or federal government on behalf of paying corporate clients.
(c) "Prohibited Contribution" means any financial donation, transfer, loan, or in-kind gift originating from a Corporate PAC or Registered Lobbyist.

Section 3. Ban on Direct Committee Receipts.
The Committee and its officers shall not solicit, accept, or deposit any Prohibited Contribution into Committee accounts. Any Prohibited Contribution delivered to the Committee shall be returned to the donor within fourteen (14) days[cite: 2].

Section 4. Candidate Endorsements and Committee Resources.
The Committee shall not endorse, financially support, or provide coordinated campaign resources to any candidate for public office who actively solicits or accepts Corporate PAC contributions during the relevant election cycle[cite: 2].

Section 5. Transparency and Inspection.
The Treasurer shall present an itemized balance sheet of all receipts and disbursements at every regularly scheduled meeting. Any member of the Committee in good standing has the right to inspect all banking records upon written notice to the Executive Committee.
