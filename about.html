About the Anti-Politician

Corey Spangler describes himself as an applied philosopher who happens to work inside the Democratic Party — because the system has been captured by a duopoly, and the machinery worth capturing back is already built.

Corey serves on the Cabot City Council (Ward 1, Position 2), a seat he has held since 2023; he was reelected on November 3, 2026. He chairs the Democratic Party of Lonoke County. His record — the rules he has actually passed, and their current status — is kept in the Living Ledger, where claims go to be checked, not believed.

What this project is / is not

It is

A reclamation project

A cross-partisan effort to move serious, unbought people into the vacant seats of local party committees and bind those committees with clean-money rules.

It is not

Not a party, not a faction

Not a Democratic project. Not a Republican project. Not another third-party vanity run at the ballot. The parties are the terrain; they are not the team.

The vocabulary, kept straight

Dynamic Synthesism
The philosophy: efficacy over aesthetic; synthesize what works, retire what does not, and write the verdict down.
The Plebeian Reclamation
The operation: audit the room, claim the empty seats, change the binding rules.
Sophistry → Stewardship
The standard: the one axis leaders are graded on — performance for the cameras, or stewardship of the public’s business.
The Living Ledger
The record: every claim this project makes, with its source, status, and date — kept current in public.
