About The Stoic Opposition

The Stoic Opposition is a cross-partisan civic reclamation project. Its lead test is Make Politics Boring Again: watch the meeting, read the minutes, check the numbers — because if politics is exciting, somebody is usually selling something.

Its method is the Plebeian Reclamation: audit the room, claim the vacant seats in local party committees, and bind those committees with clean-money rules. The lineage is Civic Pragmatism; the practice is Operational Governance. The subject is always systems and mechanisms — never villains, and never team sports. The parties are the terrain; they are not the team.

Its public record is the Living Ledger, where every claim the project makes is kept with its source, status, and date — claims checked, not believed. Its tools include the Playbook, Systems You Can Play (a free suite of playable arguments), and Premise Check, the rhetorical logic audit.

What this project is / is not

It is

A reclamation project

A cross-partisan effort to move serious, unbought people into the vacant seats of local party committees and bind those committees with clean-money rules.

It is not

Not a party, not a faction

Not a Democratic project. Not a Republican project. Not another third-party vanity run at the ballot. The parties are the terrain; they are not the team.

The vocabulary, kept straight

Make Politics Boring Again
The lead, and the test: watch a meeting, read the minutes, check the numbers. If politics is exciting, somebody is usually selling something.
Civic Pragmatism
The lineage: an existing tradition the project aligns with — not a term it coined, and not a system it is selling.
Operational Governance
The practice: efficacy over aesthetic; test what works, retire what does not, and write the verdict down.
Synthesism
The footnote: a tongue-in-cheek label from the founding manifesto. It is a joke, not a doctrine — don’t trust the word, check the record.
The Plebeian Reclamation
The operation: audit the room, claim the empty seats, change the binding rules.
Sophistry → Stewardship
The standard: the one axis leaders are graded on — performance for the cameras, or stewardship of the public’s business.
The Living Ledger
The record: every claim this project makes, with its source, status, and date — kept current in public.
