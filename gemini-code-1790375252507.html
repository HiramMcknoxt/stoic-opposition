What We're Working on in Ward 2

1. Stormwater & Infrastructure Capacity

You can't pave over hundreds of acres of topsoil without giving that water somewhere to go. We're prioritizing detention requirements, ditch maintenance, and culvert replacements so existing homes don't take on water when our weather turns rough.

2. Responsible Growth & Practical Zoning

Cabot is growing fast, but growth without infrastructure planning is just negligence. I vote to make sure new developments pay their fair share of street, water, and sewer impacts before we hook them up to the city grid.

3. Transparent Public Spending

Tax dollars belong to the people who worked for them. Every vendor contract, municipal bond, and equipment purchase should be open for any citizen in Lonoke County to inspect without jumping through hoops.
