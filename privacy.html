Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

This prototype collects and stores nothing. This page describes the rules a future roster or intake form will operate under before this project ever asks for your information for real — published first, on purpose.

What would be collected, and why

  • Name and a way to reach you (email, and optionally a phone number) — so a human can follow up about your county audit.
  • State, county, and precinct if you know it — because the entire method is geographic; your committee is determined by where you live.
  • The party you intend to work inside, and what you are willing to do (claim a seat, observe a meeting, introduce the rule, print handbills) — so the follow-up you get is the one you asked for.

Nothing else. No financial information is ever collected, because this project takes no donations.

What is never done with it

  • Never sold. Not to campaigns, not to parties, not to data brokers, not to anybody.
  • Never shared with campaigns or party organizations. Your county committee does not get your information from us; you walk into that room yourself, when you choose to.
  • Never used for donation solicitation. There are no donations.
  • Never published. The Living Ledger records mechanisms and outcomes, not volunteers’ personal information.

Retention, and your say

Information would be kept only while it is useful for the follow-up you asked for. Ask for it to be deleted and it is deleted — no retention schedule to hide behind, no “we’ll get back to you.” The same goes for corrections: if we have something wrong about you, tell us and it gets fixed.

On this prototype specifically: the tools on the Take a Seat page run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type there is transmitted or stored anywhere. Close the tab and it is gone.
