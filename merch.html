Merch + Free Stencils

Wear it. Or make it yourself — free.

These shirts exist to get the word out. They do not exist to make money.

Every design below has two doors. Door one: buy it from the shop (Redbubble — The Stoic Opposition, BSDMS) if you want a finished shirt, sticker, poster, or hoodie. Door two: skip the shop entirely. Download the stencil — the actual 3D-printable STL plate, plus the SVG — for free, print it, and spray-paint your own shirt, sign, or tote. If giving the files away gets the word out just as well, giving them away is the point.

Free stencils Fork it

Just want the free files? They have their own page now: Free Merch — every stencil plate, free to download.

The free-use line, plain: Free to print and use for personal, noncommercial shirts and signs. Fork them, remix them, share them.

Printing notes: plates are 150 mm wide (the HOPE face is 170 mm), 1.6 mm thick, island-checked so no pieces fall out. PETG flexes and survives wipe-downs; PLA works. Scale X/Y in your slicer if your bed is smaller. Full spraying notes ship with the stencil set.

Note: a handful of Democratic Party of Lonoke County designs are currently sitting in this shop from a November mix-up. They are deliberately not listed on this page — the two projects stay separate — and whether they stay in the shop at all is a separate decision, still open.
