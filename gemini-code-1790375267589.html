The Project

The Stoic Opposition

Reclaiming local party infrastructure from the bottom up[cite: 2].

The political establishment relies on regular people staying home so corporate PACs and consultants can run the show[cite: 1, 2]. We are here to change that[cite: 2].

The Stoic Opposition is a decentralized network of unbought people taking operational control of their local party committees (Republican or Democratic) and passing binding rules that ban corporate PAC and lobbyist money[cite: 1, 2]. We did it in Lonoke County, and you can do it in yours[cite: 2].
