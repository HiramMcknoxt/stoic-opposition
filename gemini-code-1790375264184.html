Aviation & Engineering

GaiusFPV & Technical Systems

Open-source avionics, PID loops, and real-world physics.

I've been building and flying FPV (First-Person View) drones for years under the callsign GaiusFPV[cite: 1].

Long before I got into local elected office, I spent late nights over a soldering iron building custom carbon-fiber quads. In a world full of politicians and consultants who make a living by spinning words, working with electronics keeps you grounded. If your solder joint is cold, or your flight controller PID loops aren't tuned properly, the quad drops out of the sky. Reality doesn't negotiate.

The Fleet & Hardware

5-Inch Freestyle Airframes

Built for high-g maneuvers, prop wash handling, and durability. Running custom 2207/2306 brushless motors on 6S LiPo power, carbon fiber unibody plates, and digital HD video transmission feeds.

7-Inch Mid-Range & Mountain Rigs

Configured for efficient cruising, GPS rescue fail-safes, and clean video signals over distance. Tuned specifically for low-RPM stability and long-range cinematic capture.

The Software Stack

I believe deeply in open-source systems, both in public governance and in technology:

  • Betaflight Firmware: Dynamic notch filtering, D-term dampening, and custom feedforward jitter reduction for locked-in flight characteristics.
  • ExpressLRS (ELRS): Open-source, high-refresh-rate RC link operating on 2.4GHz and 915MHz with low packet loss and adaptive power output.
  • OpenTX / EdgeTX: Open radio firmware customized for telemetry alerts, switch logic, and hardware diagnostics.
"There is something profoundly healthy about working on problems where you cannot talk your way out of a mistake. If it doesn't fly right, you built it wrong. Fix the solder, retune the filters, and try again."
